# Showing the ripple at the moment of revision makes nonlinear change workable

Nonlinear revision becomes workable when the ripple is shown *at the moment of revision*, with repairs proposed.

This is the project's central claim. If it fails, the project loses its novelty.

*Evidence today.* The design bet of the [nonlinear-interaction spec](../../specs/nonlinear-interaction/requirements.md); no evidence yet. Frame tracking solved parallel hypothetical states a decade ago and never got an LLM-era UI, so there is nothing to retrieve — [the gap is being answered by building](../../research/gaps.md#l3).

*How we would know it is wrong.* Ripple explanations are ignored or overwhelm, or users prefer to start a fresh configuration over repairing one.

*What rests on it.* The whole ripple storyboard ([../models/Ripple storyboard.md](../models/Ripple%20storyboard.md)), and review of delegated agent work, which reuses the same machinery ([../models/Conversation moves.md](../models/Conversation%20moves.md) §5).
