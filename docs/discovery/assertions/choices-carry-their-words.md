# A choice that carries its words can be revisited without re-litigation

A recorded choice should carry its grounds at the level of words — the customer's quoted utterance, the document's quoted clause — not merely a source category. When the grounds are one look away, revisiting a value is a decision about substance ("is 1000 kg still right?") rather than an argument about attribution ("did I ever say that?"), and delegating the rest of the sheet to the agent stays tolerable because what *was* stated is checkable.

Load-bearing for the direction's central tension: [the agent proposes; the user disposes](../principles/agent-proposes-user-disposes.md) holds against [always show a valid whole](../principles/always-show-a-valid-whole.md) through "visible provenance plus cheap reversal", and a three-way source badge is the weakest possible reading of visible provenance. This asserts the badge is not enough — the words are the working unit. It also closes a leak in [the canvas is the durable state](canvas-is-the-durable-state.md): conversations are ephemeral by design, several per workspace, so words that live only in the transcript are words the durable record has lost — the transcript-re-litigation failure of pure chat ([problem-framing](../problem-framing.md) §1, why #4) reappearing one level up.

*Evidence today.* The document channel already implements it: RFQ requirements carry clause and quote, frozen in state, rendered in the badge popover — and that is what makes the deviation register readable as the customer's own ask rather than the vendor's paraphrase ([a seeded candidate with named deviations beats compliance-checking by hand](deviations-beat-compliance-checking.md)). Tender practice agrees: a compliance matrix cites clauses; a bare "complies" is not accepted. The intent-communication literature (Kim et al., CHI 2025) names *management* — stated preferences scattering across conversation turns — as a distinct failure mode of chat-only interfaces, remedied by externalising stated intent as persistent objects. The prose channel is untested here; the [choice-provenance spec](../../specs/choice-provenance/requirements.md) exists to test it.

*How we would know it is wrong.* The popover goes unopened — the badge alone carries all the trust the customer needs, and the words are ceremony. Or extraction fidelity is too low: the agent paraphrases, and a misattributed "quote" is worse than a bare badge because it puts words in the customer's mouth (the RFQ verification already logged one invented mapping — clause 5.2 read as a requirement the document left open).

*What rests on it.* The [choice-provenance spec](../../specs/choice-provenance/requirements.md); the sharpened provenance half of the test in [the agent proposes; the user disposes](../principles/agent-proposes-user-disposes.md) — from *who chose a value* to *on what words*. The streaming generalisation — a stated-needs register diffing every channel's asks against the live agreement — waits on this holding, and on the reference-semantics question recorded in [problem-framing](../problem-framing.md) §5.
