## What's open

This is a standing list rather than a phase schedule. An item opens when new territory comes into frame, and closes when the work lands in the note or spec that carries it. Closed work isn't recorded here: it lives in the artifact it produced, and in git. What repeats is building, and every cycle of it ends in a walkthrough of the running prototype against the [scenarios](scenarios/), which reopens this list. The requirement that all four activities be represented holds over the plan as a whole rather than over any stretch of it, and the activity balance is where it's checked.

*The unexplored territory is the session map*, meaning how a returning operator's arrival surface is laid out. It has a substrate already: the [open-points spec](../specs/open-points/requirements.md) derives the agreement's remaining questions from the workspace record, and it awaits approval. Until that is built, resumption answers *what is still open* only at category grain, which is a constraint the session map inherits rather than a status note. It also inherits the first proposal's objective, settled so far only as a disclosed cheapest-completion default ([Conversation moves](models/Conversation%20moves.md), *The initiative default*, and carried as a live question by [trade-offs are shown as a pair](principles/trade-offs-shown-as-a-pair.md)). The [comparison view](models/Comparison%20view.md) gives that pair somewhere to go, so the question is answerable rather than blocked.

*The models marked drafted, for critique haven't had it*: [Canvas anatomy](models/Canvas%20anatomy.md), [Comparison view](models/Comparison%20view.md) and [Ripple storyboard](models/Ripple%20storyboard.md). Critique from a knowledgeable reader is the only substitute this project has for a design studio, and none has been arranged. The comparison view carries a second debt: it chose a canvas mode over the shipped chat card, and that decision has no spec.

*The job crossing left four openings*, recorded in the [micro-job stories](jtbd/micro-job-stories.md). The approver has two shapes, internal and external, and no artifact anywhere. Renewal's comparison is asymmetric, and has nothing in force to compare against until acceptance exists. Delegation is served only on the agent's side of the boundary. And no value can be marked as one another discipline has already built on. The note also leaves open whether the crossing should be promoted into a model of its own, since the unserved micro-jobs concentrate at the Monitor and Conclude ends the job map predicts a configurator abandons.

*Two assertions can't be settled by this project as staffed.* [The representation of the agreement selects the user's moves](assertions/representation-selects-moves.md) has two representations to compare, but the comparison needs readers who aren't the author. [Unsat cores are sufficient for trust](assertions/cores-are-sufficient-for-trust.md) can't be tested until cores reach the customer.

## Stop and continue signals

Discovery stops, for now, when:

- a cycle produces no revision to the framing or direction;
- the pull toward elaborating details is stronger than the pull toward reframing;
- the open questions in [problem-framing.md](problem-framing.md), *Open questions the framing does not settle*, are answered by decisions rather than by more reading.

Discovery continues when:

- an interaction decision can't be argued from any principle, which means the direction is incomplete;
- the same debate recurs across sessions without resolution;
- a walkthrough contradicts an assertion and nothing replaces it;
- building feels uninspired in a way that traces to weak understanding rather than to ordinary implementation difficulty.

Current read: continue. The comparison view was drawn on 2026-08-20 from three candidates, with the shipped one judged by watching it fail in the app rather than by argument. But a drafted model isn't a validated one, and its own failure signals name four ways it could be wrong, none of them yet tested.

The 2026-08-20 prototype walkthrough doesn't change the activity-balance verdict: walking the prototype produced first-party evidence about the built interaction, which is gathering, and the presentation decisions behind what it found were still taken one alternative at a time. What it did change is where the pressure sits. Most of what it found was a defect in built work rather than a gap in the framing, and repairing those isn't discovery — it is the walkthrough paying for itself, which is the argument for running one after every cycle of building. That the state model was decided in argument, from a spec conversation rather than from a drawing, is the same diagnosis repeating, and it is unchanged. The session map hasn't been started, and it carries the first proposal's objective with it.
