# Job ladder — from tool task to main job

Status: desk-research hypothesis. Plays: *Scope the JTBD Domain*, for laddering, and *Expand Market Opportunities*, for "ask why twice, then ask what business you are really in".

Moving up from the current solution frame:

1. "Configure an elevator" — solution language, and the tool's task rather than anyone's job.
2. "Equip the building with vertical transportation that meets its traffic, code, space and budget needs" — the job a *product* configurator serves. It ends at handover.
3. Why? To "keep people and goods moving safely, reliably and affordably through the building over its life."
4. Why? To "keep the building operational, compliant and attractive to its occupants."

Level 3 is the main job at the right altitude ([domain.md](domain.md)). Level 2 is a *phase* of level 3 that ends the relationship just as the performer's struggle begins, in operation, adaptation and renewal ([job-map.md](job-map.md)).

## What the ladder decides

This is the argument the [elevator-as-a-service offering](../../specs/service-agreement/requirements.md) rests on: the job is stable, product against service is a choice of solution form, and the service form covers more of the job. It is also what relocated the user — under the service frame the [building operator](persona-building-operator.md) becomes the primary persona, and revision the primary interaction ([problem framing](../problem-framing.md), *Contextual statements*).
