# Job stories — the demo scenarios in the service frame

Status: desk-research hypothesis. Play: *Align Teams to Job Stories*. Aligned to the [demo scenarios](../../specs/demo-scenarios/requirements.md), recast from the product frame into the service frame; [direction.md](../direction.md) §4 maps each story to the principles it demonstrates and the assertions it exercises.

1. *Needs, not nomenclature.* When I am planning a medical building and know beds and floors but nothing about elevators, I want to state my needs in building terms and receive a guaranteed-performance proposal, so I can lock shaft dimensions early without becoming an elevator expert. (Holds under the service frame; the proposal carries outcome terms and a monthly price. Personas: [design specifier](persona-design-specifier.md), [delivery lead](persona-delivery-lead.md).)
2. *Mid-contract revision.* When tenant complaints about wait times rise after my building's use changed, I want to see what different outcome terms would cost per month and what they ripple into, so I can fix the problem without a capital project. (Revision-with-repair, recast from "change the order" to "change the agreement". Persona: [building operator](persona-building-operator.md).)
3. *Comparing agreements.* When I must choose a service level, I want two candidate agreements side by side with the monthly-price delta and exactly what differs, so the trade-off is explicit. (Parallel candidates, recast as service-tier comparison.)
4. *Renewal as revision.* When renewal approaches, I want to resume from the live configuration and adjust it to how the building is actually used, so renewal is a revision, not a new sale. (Resumption, recast with a why: the durable canvas is now a durable *contract*.)
5. *Tender as entrance.* When our project has issued an RFQ for the vertical transportation package, I want the response seeded from our own document — what it can meet recorded as commitments, what it cannot named as deviations with their rules — so we negotiate the differences instead of re-answering what we already wrote. (The document door, [problem-framing.md](../problem-framing.md) §3; acquisition and revision as one document's life. Persona: [delivery lead](persona-delivery-lead.md).)
