# Job stories

Status: desk-research hypothesis. Play: *Align Teams to Job Stories*. Each story localizes the [job map](job-map.md) at a point in the [consumption journey](consumption-journey.md) and specializes a persona, and each is grounded by the scenario of the same name in [scenarios/](../scenarios/), which plays it through as a concrete path.

These five were recast from an earlier product-frame set of walkthroughs when the service frame landed, which is why the scenarios carry their names. The dependency runs from here: a story states the situation, the motivation and the outcome, and the scenario is one way of making that visible.

1. *Needs, not nomenclature.* When I am planning a medical building and know beds and floors but nothing about elevators, I want to state my needs in building terms and receive a guaranteed-performance proposal, so I can lock shaft dimensions early without becoming an elevator expert. It holds under the service frame, where the proposal carries outcome terms and a monthly price. Personas: [design specifier](persona-design-specifier.md), [delivery lead](persona-delivery-lead.md).
2. *Mid-contract revision.* When tenant complaints about wait times rise after my building's use changed, I want to see what different outcome terms would cost per month and what they ripple into, so I can fix the problem without a capital project. This is revision with repair, recast from "change the order" to "change the agreement". Persona: [building operator](persona-building-operator.md).
3. *Comparing agreements.* When I have to choose a service level, I want two candidate agreements side by side with the monthly-price delta and exactly what differs, so the trade-off is explicit. This is parallel candidates, recast as service-tier comparison.
4. *Renewal as revision.* When renewal approaches, I want to resume from the live configuration and adjust it to how the building is actually used, so renewal is a revision rather than a new sale. This is resumption, recast with a why: the durable canvas is now a durable *contract*.
5. *Tender as entrance.* When our project has issued an RFQ for the vertical transportation package, I want the response seeded from our own document — what it can meet recorded as commitments, what it can't named as deviations with their rules — so we negotiate the differences instead of re-answering what we already wrote. This is the document as entry point ([problem framing](../problem-framing.md), *Contextual statements*), with acquisition and revision as one document's life. Persona: [delivery lead](persona-delivery-lead.md).
