# Domain — main job and performer

Status: desk-research hypothesis. Play: *Scope the JTBD Domain*.

*Main job statement:* keep people and goods moving safely, reliably, and affordably through a building over its life.

The statement sits at the third level of the [job ladder](job-ladder.md) — broad enough that a machine purchase is just one strategy for it, specific enough to act on, and free of solution language.

*Job performer.* The person responsible for a building's vertical transportation. Who that is depends on the building's phase:

- during delivery, the developer's or general contractor's project lead — [persona-delivery-lead.md](persona-delivery-lead.md)
- during operation, the building owner or facility manager — [persona-building-operator.md](persona-building-operator.md)

*Influencers, buyers, approvers — not performers.* Architects specify ([persona-design-specifier.md](persona-design-specifier.md)), elevator consultants advise, boards approve and pay. The scoping play insists on keeping these separate from the performer, because the evidence about the job sits with whoever executes it.
