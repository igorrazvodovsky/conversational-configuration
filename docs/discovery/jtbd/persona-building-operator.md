# Persona — the building operator

Status: desk-research hypothesis. Play: *Create Goal-Based Personas*, defined by goals and circumstances rather than demographics.

The owner or facility manager of an operating building. They are the job performer during the operation phase ([domain.md](domain.md)), and *the primary persona under the service frame*.

*Goals.* Uptime, predictable operating cost, tenant satisfaction, no capital surprises.

*Circumstances.* Aging equipment, opex-preferred budgeting, and board approval needed for capital projects.

*Frustrations.* Opaque maintenance contracts, downtime disputes, and fighting for modernization capex.

## Why this persona reframes the design

Their job — Monitor, Modify, Conclude ([job-map.md](job-map.md)) — is mostly *revision of something that already exists*, and first-time configuration is the atypical case. This is what makes revision the primary interaction rather than an edge case ([problem framing](../problem-framing.md), *Contextual statements*: the design target is the reviser rather than the newcomer), and it grounds [revision is an ordinary move, not a restart](../principles/revision-is-an-ordinary-move.md). The product-frame prototype underserved this persona, because only the modernization demo touched them, and it still ended in a purchase.
