# Persona — the building operator

Status: desk-research hypothesis. Play: *Create Goal-Based Personas* — defined by goals and circumstances, not demographics.

Owner or facility manager of an operating building. The job performer during the operation phase ([domain.md](domain.md)) — and *the primary persona under the service frame*.

*Goals.* Uptime, predictable operating cost, tenant satisfaction, no capital surprises.

*Circumstances.* Aging equipment; opex-preferred budgeting; board approval needed for capital projects.

*Frustrations.* Opaque maintenance contracts, downtime disputes, fighting for modernization capex.

## Why this persona reframes the design

Their job — Monitor, Modify, Conclude ([job-map.md](job-map.md)) — is mostly *revision of something that already exists*. First-time configuration is the atypical case. This is what makes revision the primary interaction rather than an edge case ([../problem-framing.md](../problem-framing.md) §3: the design target is the reviser, not the newcomer) and grounds [revision is an ordinary move, not a restart](../principles/revision-is-an-ordinary-move.md). The product-frame prototype underserved this persona: only the modernization demo touched them, and it still ended in a purchase.
