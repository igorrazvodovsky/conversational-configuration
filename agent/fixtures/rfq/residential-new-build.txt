MERIDIAN HOUSING ASSOCIATION
REQUEST FOR QUOTATION — RFQ 2026/HV-114

Vertical transportation, availability-based service agreement
Riverfield Court, Block C — new residential development

Issued: 3 March 2026
Responses due: 24 March 2026, 17:00 CET
Contact: procurement@meridian-housing.example


1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 Meridian Housing Association invites quotations for the supply,
installation and ongoing service of one passenger lift serving Block C
of the Riverfield Court development.

1.2 We are procuring vertical transportation as a service. Bidders shall
quote a monthly charge covering equipment, installation, maintenance and
the availability commitments in section 4. Bids expressed as a capital
purchase price will be returned unread.

1.3 This is a new-build project. The shaft is being constructed to the
successful bidder's setting-out drawings; no existing equipment is being
retained.


2. THE BUILDING

2.1 Riverfield Court Block C is a residential apartment building. The
lift serves residents and their visitors only; no commercial or public
access is provided.

2.2 The building is located in the Netherlands. All equipment, testing
and documentation shall comply with the European regulatory regime.

2.3 Travel height from the lowest served level to the highest is 24
metres. The number of served levels is subject to confirmation against
the final architectural set (issue D, expected mid-March) and bidders
should state their assumption.

2.4 Expected traffic is steady residential use through the day, with
morning and evening peaks. The building has 46 apartments.


3. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Rated load shall be not less than 1000 kg (13 persons). This figure
is driven by our furniture-movement policy and is not negotiable
downward.

3.2 Rated speed shall be 1.6 m/s.

3.3 The lift shall be accessible in accordance with EN 81-70. Meridian's
allocation policy places tenants with mobility needs in this block and
accessibility compliance is a condition of our funding.


4. SERVICE AND AVAILABILITY

4.1 The service agreement shall provide 24/7 call-out cover.

4.2 Response time to a reported failure shall be no longer than 8 hours.

4.3 Contracted availability shall be no less than 99.5 % measured over a
rolling twelve-month period.

4.4 Entrapment release shall be attended within the response time in 4.2.
Bidders shall describe their release procedure.


5. COMMERCIAL

5.1 Quotations shall state a single monthly charge per lift, inclusive of
all items in section 1.2. The charge shall not exceed EUR 1,200 per lift
per month.

5.2 The agreement term is open to proposal. Bidders should state the term
their price assumes and may quote alternative terms.

5.3 Handover is required by 30 November 2026. Liquidated damages of EUR
250 per day apply beyond this date.

5.4 Prices shall be held firm for 90 days from the response date.


6. RESPONSE FORMAT

6.1 Bidders shall respond clause by clause, stating compliance,
partial compliance or non-compliance against each numbered requirement,
with an explanation for anything other than full compliance.

6.2 Any assumption made in place of information this document does not
provide shall be stated explicitly.

— End of RFQ 2026/HV-114 —
