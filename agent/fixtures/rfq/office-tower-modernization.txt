HALDANE ESTATES GmbH
REQUEST FOR QUOTATION — RFQ MOD-2026-07

Lift modernization and service agreement
Kranhaus Nord, Bank Tower — car no. 3 (low-rise-to-top shuttle)

Issued: 11 February 2026
Responses due: 6 March 2026, 12:00 CET
Contact: r.velden@haldane-estates.example


1. BACKGROUND

1.1 Kranhaus Nord is a 1987 office tower under a phased refurbishment.
The lift group is being modernized car by car; this RFQ covers car no. 3.

1.2 The works are a modernization within the existing shaft. The shaft,
pit and machine room are as built in 1987 and are not being altered:
the structural works package for this phase does not extend below the
lowest landing or above the existing overrun, and no permission has been
sought for either.

1.3 As with the rest of the group, the replacement is procured as a
service. Bidders shall quote a monthly charge over the contract term
covering equipment, installation, maintenance and the performance
commitments in section 5. Capital purchase quotations are not sought.


2. THE BUILDING

2.1 Kranhaus Nord is a commercial office building, let to eleven
tenants. Ground-floor reception is public; the tower floors are
tenant-access.

2.2 The building is in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. European codes apply.

2.3 Car no. 3 travels 92 metres from the ground lobby to level 24.

2.4 The car serves 18 landings.

2.5 Following the refurbishment the building will be at full occupancy,
approximately 2,100 people. Car no. 3 is the group's principal shuttle
and is in near-constant demand from 07:00 until the building empties.


3. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Rated speed shall be 3.0 m/s. Our traffic study (Voss & Partner,
November 2025, appended separately) sets this figure as the requirement
for a five-minute handling capacity of 12 % at the morning peak.

3.2 Rated load shall be 1600 kg (21 persons).

3.3 The lift shall be accessible in accordance with EN 81-70, consistent
with the rest of the modernized group.


4. TENANT AND LEGAL COMMITMENTS

4.1 Two tenant leases signed in 2025 contain lift performance schedules
that this specification is written to satisfy. Bidders should assume
these commitments cannot be varied without tenant consent.

4.2 The building's accessibility certification is being renewed on
completion of the phase.


5. SERVICE AND AVAILABILITY

5.1 The service agreement shall provide 24/7 call-out cover with remote
monitoring and diagnostics.

5.2 Contracted availability shall be no less than 99.9 % measured over a
rolling twelve-month period.

5.3 Response time to a reported failure shall be no longer than 2 hours
during building hours.

5.4 Availability below the level in 5.2 in any two consecutive months
shall trigger a service credit of 15 % of the monthly charge.

5.5 The contract term shall be 15 years, coterminous with the building's
refurbishment financing.


6. COMMERCIAL

6.1 The monthly charge shall not exceed EUR 1,800 per lift per month.

6.2 Cars 4 and 5 are expected to follow in 2027 on the same terms.
Bidders may state a group rate but shall price this RFQ standalone.

6.3 Possession of the shaft is available from 4 May 2026 for a maximum
of 14 weeks. The building remains occupied throughout.


7. RESPONSE FORMAT

7.1 Bidders shall respond clause by clause, stating compliance, partial
compliance or non-compliance against each numbered requirement.

7.2 Any deviation from a numbered requirement shall be stated as a
deviation, with the reason and the alternative offered. Silent
substitution is grounds for disqualification.

— End of RFQ MOD-2026-07 —
